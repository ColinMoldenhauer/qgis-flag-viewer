about=Flag Viewer samples the raster band or mesh dataset group value under the mouse cursor and decodes it into its CF flag_meanings, using flag_values/flag_masks metadata auto-detected from the file, or a user-supplied JSON/YAML mapping. Supports both "categorized" flags (one value = one meaning) and "bitmask" flags (each bit = one meaning).
